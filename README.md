# VoocHealth

![Platforms](https://img.shields.io/badge/platforms-iOS%20%7C%20Android%20%7C%20HarmonyOS-333333)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/github/license/caphefalumi/huawei-fitwear)](LICENSE)

Your workouts, meals, and recovery in one place.

VoocHealth is a fitness companion for your phone, with a HarmonyOS watch app for wearable workouts. Keep a record of your training and nutrition, and see your progress over time.

## Train with a plan

Track workout sessions, exercises, sets, and weights. Explore exercise technique guides as you train and revisit your workout history.

## Make meals easier to track

Log meals and portions and follow your daily nutrition. Add foods manually, scan a packaged-food barcode, or use a photo to get an estimate you can review and edit before logging.

## Follow your progress

Keep your weight, profile, and recovery schedule alongside your training and nutrition records. Your account keeps your history available across sessions.

## On your wrist

The VoocHealth watch app brings the experience to HarmonyOS watches, with access to movement and heart-rate sensors and haptic feedback.

Meal photo estimates are provided as guidance, not measurements.
