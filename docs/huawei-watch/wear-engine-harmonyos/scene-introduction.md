# Use Cases

#### Distributed Collaboration Between Phones and Wearables

- Important notifications can be delivered directly to the wearable, enabling instant reminders without opening the phone. Reminders set in the phone-side app, such as schedule reminders, medication reminders, and task reminders, can be synchronized to the wearable-side app, allowing users to view essential information at any time.
- The "wrist-based interaction" capability enhances the phone-side app experience. When users watch videos or listen to audio in the phone-side app, the wearable-side app can control playback, such as pausing, skipping to the next track, or stopping, directly from the wearable.
- A real-time collaborative experience is provided. For example, when navigation is active in the phone-side app, the wearable-side app can display turn prompts, including left, straight, or right. In this way, users can follow directions while walking or cycling without needing to take out their phone.

#### Virtualization Between Phones and Wearables

You can develop a phone-side app without having to develop a corresponding app for the wearable.

- Real-time awareness of the wearable status: The phone-side app can obtain real-time status information of the wearable, such as the connection status, wearing status, and battery level, enabling more value-added services for users.
- Sensor of the wearable: The phone-side app can read sensor data (such as ECG, PPG, and ACC data) from the wearable. > **Note:** The sensors can only be used by professional research institutions due to the limited release.
