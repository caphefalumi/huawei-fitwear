# Service Advantages

Wear Engine allows apps to be quickly and conveniently connected to Huawei wearable devices. This opens the possibility for you to explore and provides users with a broader range of application scenarios, an easier-to-use service experience, and seamless interaction with wearable devices.

- **Convenient management**: Makes cross-device collaboration a full-fledged reality, based on device connection and real-time status monitoring.
- **Real-time reminders**: Allows for timely reminders for important events sent directly to the wrist.
- **New ways of interacting**: Introduces the brand new interactive experiences to the wrist, such as using a wearable device to control music playback and navigate.
