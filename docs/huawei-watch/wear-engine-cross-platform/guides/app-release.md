# App Release

#### Reviewing Guidelines
Before releasing your app, read the following for the guidelines and FAQs you may need to know:

- [AppGallery Review Guidelines](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/devservice/doc/50104)
- [App Audit FAQ](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/devservice/doc/50106)

#### Submitting Your App

When your app is ready for release, submit an application in AppGallery Connect. For details, refer to:

- Releasing an app: [Releasing an Android app](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/distribution/app/agc-help-releaseapkrpk-0000001106463276)
- Releasing a HarmonyOS wearable app: [Releasing a HarmonyOS App](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/app/agc-help-release-app-0000002271695230) > **Note:** If you have not passed identity verification or have passed identity verification but have not been invited to use HarmonyOS-related services, you can only access functions for developing and debugging HarmonyOS apps but cannot perform HarmonyOS release operations described in this section. If you have verified your identity and want to release HarmonyOS apps, send your name and ID registered at HUAWEI Developers, the reason for your request, and device types that your app supports to agconnect@huawei.com. Huawei operations personnel will contact you within one to three working days. If you have not passed identity verification, complete identity verification first. To find your ID, sign in to AppGallery Connect, click **My projects**, click any project card, and go to **Project settings** > **General information**. You will find the ID in the **Developer information** area.
