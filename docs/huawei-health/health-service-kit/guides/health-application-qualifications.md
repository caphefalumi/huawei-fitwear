# Qualifications Requirements for Developers

#### For Individual Developers

- The app should have been released on AppGallery. Otherwise, Health Service Kit data will not be open to the app.
- Individual developers who apply for accessing user data with the basic openness level should not have any adverse credit records. User data with the advanced openness level is currently not open to individual developers. For details about the data openness levels, please refer to [Open Data Overview](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/health-data-overview).

#### For Developers from Enterprises, Governments, and Social Organizations

- For enterprises: For enterprises outside Europe, Japan, and the Chinese mainland: To apply for accessing user data with the basic openness level, the paid-up share capital of the enterprise must be CNY500,000 or greater; to apply for accessing user data with the advanced openness level, the paid-up share capital of the enterprise must be CNY5 million or greater. The application entity must have been established and existed for more than one year.
- For enterprises in Europe: To apply for accessing user data with the basic openness level, the paid-up share capital of the enterprise must be EUR25,000 or greater, and the application entity must have been established and existed for more than one year; to apply for accessing user data with the advanced openness level, the paid-up share capital of the enterprise must be EUR25,000 or greater, and the application entity must have been established and existed for more than two years.
- For enterprises in Japan: To apply for accessing user data with the basic openness level, the paid-up share capital of the enterprise must be JPY10 million or greater; to apply for accessing user data with the advanced openness level, the paid-up share capital of the enterprise must be JPY10 million or greater. The application entity must have been established and existed for more than one year.

- For governments and social organizations: Application entities, such as governments, academic and research institutions, medical institutions, and public insurance institutions, can be exempted from requirements on capital and years of establishment. Government-entrusted enterprises must comply with the requirements for enterprises.
- For non-enterprise entities: Subject to the capital requirements specified in local laws.

> **Note:** Enterprise developers must provide official documents (such as official annual reports, audit reports, articles of association, capital verification reports, and registration documents): The provided documents must record the amount of share capital and whether the share capital is paid-up. The supporting documents must be attached in the application materials. For non-English documents, please mark the location where the paid-up share capital is recorded. If you have any questions about the qualification requirements, [submit a ticket](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/support/feedback/#/) or send an email to [hihealth@huawei.com](mailto:hihealth@huawei.com). Specify the following in your email or ticket: developer site, country/region where users reside, introduction to the enterprise and project scenarios, and contact information of the project owner.

#### Application Material Preparation

To ensure user privacy and data security, Health Service Kit requires that you provide related materials and information to ensure that the data scopes requested by your app are reasonable. Prepare and fill in the application materials based on the **application material example** on the **app information submission** page.

> **Note:** Please read [Common Issues That Lead to Request Rejection](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/health-apply#common-issues-that-lead-to-request-rejection) before submitting materials for application to avoid similar problems in your materials. If you need to apply for [app transfer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/app/agc-help-introduction-0000002270860209), Health Service Kit will review the qualification of the transferee to ensure that the new app meets the qualification requirements. Send the preceding application materials, the app ID, app name, transferee ID, and updated app privacy policy to hihealth@huawei.com. After the qualification review is passed, you can transfer the app.
