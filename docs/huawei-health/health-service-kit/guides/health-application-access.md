# Integration Process

| Step | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Get started | 1. [Register as a developer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/start/registration-and-verification-0000001053628148), [complete identity verification](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/start/rna-0000001062530373), and [create a HarmonyOS app or atomic service](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/app/agc-help-createharmonyapp-0000001945392297). 2. [Apply for Health Service Kit](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/health-apply), select a product type, and enable the read and write permissions for the corresponding data. a. [Check your qualifications](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/health-application-qualifications). Ensure that you have met all of the requirements specified before applying for permissions. b. Select permissions based on your data usage scenario, and provide the required materials and enterprise introduction as instructed in [Applying for Health Service Kit](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/health-apply). The permission request is expected to be reviewed within 10 working days. |
| Develop your app | Complete app development as required. |
| Apply for verification | After completing the development and testing, submit a verification request. Provide the required proof video and checklist by referring to [Applying for Formal Permissions](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/health-verification). After receiving your request, we will complete the review and enable the formal permissions for your app within 15 working days. Once approved, your app can be put into large-scale commercial use. |

> **Note:** Please refer to [Qualification Requirements for Developers](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/health-application-qualifications) and [Common Issues That Lead to Request Rejection](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/health-apply#common-issues-that-lead-to-request-rejection) before submitting your test permission request. During the test phase, your app is limited to a maximum of 100 users. This limit will be lifted once your verification request is approved. The developer level (premium/key/strategic) will be determined based on the actual project collaboration situation. Health Service Kit does not support the openness of children's data.
