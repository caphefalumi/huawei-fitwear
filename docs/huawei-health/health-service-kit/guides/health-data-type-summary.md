# Overview

Health Service Kit provides a range of fitness and health data, which falls into three categories:

- Sampling data
- Workout record data
- Health record data

#### Sampling Data

Health Service Kit provides a group of public sampling data types, including:

- Daily activities
- Health sampling

#### Workout Record Data

Data of workout records that last for a period of time, such as running and cycling records, comprises the workout type, data source, start and end time, associated statistics, and associated details.

For details about workout record data and each workout type, please refer to Workout Record Data.

#### Health Record Data

Data of health records that are measured within a period of time, such as sleep records, comprises the health record type, data source, start and end time, and associated statistics and details.

For details about health record data and each type of health record, please refer to Health Record Data.
