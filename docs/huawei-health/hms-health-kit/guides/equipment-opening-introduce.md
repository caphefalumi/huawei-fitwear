# Device-oriented Open Services

For smart device developers in the fitness and health field, Health Kit provides Gym Profile and Wellness Profile to connect to hardware products of ecosystem partners and implement scientific fitness and health management scenarios, such as intelligent device interconnection, data sync, and data recording, bringing advanced technical experience to consumers. Gym Profile and Wellness Profile use the standard Bluetooth protocol. After the user links their devices, they can establish secure Bluetooth transmission channels with hardware products of ecosystem partners for data transmission.
