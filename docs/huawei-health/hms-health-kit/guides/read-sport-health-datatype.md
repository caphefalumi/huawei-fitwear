# Data Openness of Huawei Wear and Health Apps

You can use the open capabilities of Health Kit to obtain fitness and health data generated by Huawei Wear and Health.

#### Restrictions

- The Health Kit SDK version must be 5.0.4.300 or later. For details, see [Version Change History](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides-V5/version-change-history-0000001057072287-V5).
- The HUAWEI Health app version on the user's phone must be 11.0.0.512 or later.
- The user must authorize Health Kit to access data in the Health app. For details about the development work on the client and server, see [Health App Authorization](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides-V5/add-permissions-0000001050069726-V5) and [Example of the Health App Authorization](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides-V5/server-app-auth-example-0000001071757151-V5).

The table below lists the types of raw data that your app can obtain – using **SampleSet** – from the Health app. Such data is uploaded by wearables to the Health app.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Step count | com.huawei.continuous.steps.delta | DT_CONTINUOUS_STEPS_DELTA | Can be obtained from phones, watches, and smart bands. |
| Distance | com.huawei.continuous.distance.delta | DT_CONTINUOUS_DISTANCE_DELTA | Can be obtained from phones, watches, and smart bands. |
| Calories burnt | com.huawei.continuous.calories.burnt | DT_CONTINUOUS_CALORIES_BURNT | Can be obtained from phones, watches, and smart bands. |
| Heart rate | com.huawei.instantaneous.heart_rate | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_HEART_RATE | Can be obtained from watches and smart bands. |
| Sleep state | com.huawei.continuous.sleep.fragment | DT_CONTINUOUS_SLEEP | Can be obtained from watches and smart bands. |
| Blood oxygen saturation | com.huawei.instantaneous.spo2 | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_SPO2 | Can be obtained from high-end watches and smart bands. |
| Weight | com.huawei.instantaneous.body_weight | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_BODY_WEIGHT | Can be obtained from Huawei and other compatible body fat scales. |
| Blood glucose | com.huawei.instantaneous.blood_glucose | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_BLOOD_GLUCOSE | Can be obtained from compatible blood glucose meters. |
| Blood pressure | com.huawei.instantaneous.blood_pressure | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_BLOOD_PRESSURE | Can be obtained from compatible blood pressure monitors. |
| Stress | com.huawei.instantaneous.stress | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_STRESS | Can be obtained from watches and high-end smart bands. |
| Medium- and high-intensity | com.huawei.continuous.exercise_intensity.v2 | DT_CONTINUOUS_EXERCISE_INTENSITY_V2 | Can be obtained from phones, watches, and smart bands. |
| Resting heart rate | com.huawei.instantaneous.resting_heart_rate | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_RESTING_HEART_RATE | Can be obtained from watches and smart bands. |
| Jump | com.huawei.continuous.jump | DT_CONTINUOUS_JUMP | Can be obtained from some watches and smart bands while playing basketball. |
| Heart rate during workout | com.huawei.instantaneous.exercise_heart_rate | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_EXERCISE_HEART_RATE | Can be obtained from some watches and smart bands during workout. |
| Body temperature | com.huawei.instantaneous.body.temperature | DT_INSTANTANEOUS_BODY_TEMPERATURE | Can be obtained from some watches. |
