# Applying for Health Kit

Before applying for Health Kit, [register a HUAWEI ID and apply for the HUAWEI ID service](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides-V5/apply-id-0000001050069756-V5).

- Click **Health Kit**. > **Note:** If the **Health Kit** card can't be found, click **Customize console** in the upper right corner to add it.

- Click **Apply for Health Kit**, agree to the agreement, and the screen for data permission application is displayed.
- Select the required data access permissions. When adding Health Kit, comply with the least privilege principle. The data access permissions must be consistent with the business. Do not apply for any permission that is beyond the business scope or is not yet needed. After the related app or service is released, Huawei will conduct random checks on the use of permissions. The checks include but are not limited to spot checks on released apps, monitoring of API calling, and verification by assigned specialists. The verification standards and how the verification results are handled are subject to the cooperation agreement signed by you before applying for Health Kit.
- Provide the application information. To ensure user privacy and data security, Health Kit requires that you provide related materials and information to ensure that the data permissions required by your app are reasonable. 
| No. | Item | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Data usage | Provide description of the purpose and method of using each data permission that is applied for. You can provide UI screenshots (or UI design) of your app to accelerate the application approval, and ensure that the third-party information contained in the screenshots has been authorized and approved by the right holder. |
| 2 | User authorization and data display paths | Provide screenshots of the UI related to user authorization and the descriptions (including the UI for granting and modifying permissions). |
| 3 | Whether to display the HUAWEI logo on the authorization screen | If you need to use the logo, provide the screenshot (or UI design) of the logo screen in the file of the user authorization and data display paths. NOTE: The HUAWEI logo can be displayed only on the authorization screen. |
| 4 | Link to the user privacy policy | Provide the link to the privacy policy of your app. The link must be publicly accessible. |
| 5 | Link to the user agreement | Provide the link to the user agreement of your app. The link must be publicly accessible. |
| 6 | Official website address of your enterprise/app | Provide the official website address of your enterprise or app. |
| 7 | Email | If some materials are missing in your application, we will let you know by email. |

- Apply for the test permission. The application for non-restricted data permissions only will be approved quickly, while the application for restricted data permissions (indicated by a lock icon) will go through a manual review, which takes about 15 working days. The approval result will be sent to you by email. Due to data caching, perform the test 24 hours after the test permission is granted. Application will fail if the submitted materials do not meet the requirements. Update your materials based on the rejection reason in the email and submit the application again. Any question, provide your feedback via the [intelligent customer service assistant](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/customerService/#/bot-dev-top/faq-top/faq-talk-top). Once the application is approved, your app is granted the test permission and can call the corresponding API to obtain the Health Kit data for testing and development. > **Note:** Only 100 users are allowed to obtain the Health Kit data after the test permission has been granted to your app. To remove this restriction, go to step 6.
- Apply for review and verification. After your app is developed, tested, and verified, click **Application Verification** to submit the materials for review, which takes about 15 working days. After the application is approved, your app can access Health Kit. The approval result will be sent to you by email. Application will fail if the submitted materials do not meet the requirements. Update your materials based on the rejection reason in the email and submit the application again. Any question, provide your feedback via the [intelligent customer service assistant](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/customerService/#/bot-dev-top/faq-top/faq-talk-top). > **Note:** You are not allowed to modify app information or data permissions when your app is pending verification. If the application is rejected because the review materials do not meet the requirements, the application status changes to **The test permission has been granted**. You can modify the application based on the rejection reason and apply for review and verification again. For details about the acceptance standards for app release, see [Basic Function Acceptance.xlsx](https://communityfile-drcn.op.hicloud.com/FileServer/getFile/cmtyPub/011/111/111/0000000000011111111.20210716103832.01898053137235094171383770071627:50520715024133:2800:63A0B9A51F1A0B4E7204212792EA03CF747ABD82A9279A59EA9B929BF7FF5DFB.xlsx?needInitFileName=true).
- Manage permissions. If your app services change, click **management** to update the permissions accordingly and submit the application again.
