# Applying for Health Kit

- Click the Health Kit card.Figure 1 **Health Kit card** > **Note:** If the Health Kit card can't be found, click Customize console in the upper right corner to add it.

- Click Apply for Health Kit, agree to the agreement, and the screen for data permission application is displayed. For details about the types of permissions to be applied for, see [Scopes](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/development/HMSCore-Guides-V5/scope-0000001053805684-V5).Figure 2 **Applying for Health Kit**
- Enter the product information, select the required data permissions, and submit the application. When adding Health Kit, make sure the data access permissions must be consistent with the business. Do not apply for any permission that is beyond the business scope or is not yet needed. After the related app or service is released, Huawei will conduct random checks on the use of permissions. The checks include but are not limited to spot checks on released apps, monitoring of API calling, and verification by assigned specialists. The verification standards and how the verification results are handled are subject to the cooperation agreement signed by you before applying for Health Kit. Figure 3 **Applying for data permissions**
- Provide the application information. Provide the required application materials and information to prove that the data permission request is legitimate. This helps us ensure user privacy, data security, and data availability. The required information includes, but is not limited to, the following listed items. Please send the information in an email to hihealth@huawei.com, with the subject starting with [Applying for Health Kit open permissions]. 
| No. | Application Materials | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | App name, app ID, app type (mobile app), and release region. | The information is used for Huawei to determine where the app will be released and how it will be provided. |
| 2 | (Applicable to enterprise developers) Enterprise introduction, including a scanned copy of the business license, brief introduction to the enterprise and the business scope, and official website. | The information is used to confirm that the enterprise is providing services related to the fitness and health field and has sufficient operating capabilities to provide stable and reliable services for consumers. |
| 3 | App information, including the app introduction, app screenshots to showcase its major functions, and its score in HUAWEI AppGallery. | The information will help Huawei confirm that the app does require the Health Kit data and learn about the use scenarios of the app. |
| 4 | Privacy statement and user agreement that are open to users, such as the public links. | Huawei will check the privacy statement and user agreement to see how user data will be protected when using the app and whether users are informed of how their data will be stored, transmitted, and handled. |
| 5 | Screenshots of the UI related to user authorization and the descriptions (including the UI for granting and modifying permissions). | The information is used to determine that the users' authorization experience can be ensured, and they are fully informed of where the data they have authorized will be used and how to cancel the authorization. |
| 6 | Purpose of applying for permissions of restricted data types and how the data will be used. | The information is used to determine that the read/write permissions for restricted data types are indeed necessary and the minimum scope has been applied for, to prevent sensitive data from being abused or misused. |
| 7 | Qualification credentials or materials (such as medical device registration certificate and medical device business license) depending on the app release regions and the involved data types. | This information is used for Huawei to determine whether the data to be written by the app will be legal and valid in its release regions. |

- Wait while your application is under approval. The application will be handled within five working days. The actual time required depends on the requested permission types and the app/service release regions. An application won't be approved if the submitted materials do not meet the requirements. Once the application is approved, you can start to develop your app. If your business scope changes and you need to modify the corresponding data permissions, click Modify to submit your application again. The data permissions available to your app/service are the ones specified in the new approved application. Figure 4 **Application approved
