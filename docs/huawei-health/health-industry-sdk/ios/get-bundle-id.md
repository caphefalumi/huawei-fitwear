# Obtaining a Bundle ID

Before applying for a certificate, you need to provide your app bundle ID. You can obtain it in the following way:

- In Xcode, choose **Project** (root directory of the project) at the top of the navigation pane.
- In the **Targets** list in the middle, select your app target.
- Click the **Signing & Capabilities** tab.
- Obtain the app bundle ID from **Bundle Identifier**.
