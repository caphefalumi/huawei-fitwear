# Registering a HUAWEI ID and Applying for the HUAWEI ID Service

- Register as a developer and complete identity verification. For details, please refer to [HUAWEI ID Registration](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/start/registration-and-verification-0000001053628148).
- Sign in to the [HUAWEI Developers](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/) website and click **Console**.
- Go to **App services** > **Distribution and promotion** > **AppGallery Connect**. > **Note:** If the **AppGallery Connect** card can't be found, click on the right of **Distribution and promotion** to add a card.
- Click **Development and services** to go to the project page. Click **Add project**.
- Enter the project name and click **Finish**.
- Add an app to the project. Select **iOS**, and enter the app package ID ([bundle ID](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/huaweihealth-Guides/get-bundle-id-0000002594269386)) and app name.
- Click **OK** to complete the creation of the iOS app. Then, [apply for the Health Industry SDK service](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/huaweihealth-Guides/apply-for-health-industry-sdk-service-0000002594109482).
