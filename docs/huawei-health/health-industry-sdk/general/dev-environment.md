# Development Environment

#### HarmonyOS 5 or Later

- DevEco Studio 5.0.3.910 or later
- Requirements for your app:compatibleSdkVersion: 5.0.0(12)

#### HarmonyOS 3.1, 4.0 or Earlier

- DevEco Studio 3.0.0.800 to 3.1.0.501
- Requirements for your app:compileSdkVersion: 7
- compatibleSdkVersion: 6

#### Android Phone

- Android Studio 2024.2.1 or later
- Requirements for your app:minSdkVersion 24 or later
- compileSdk 35

#### iPhone

- Xcode 15 or later
- Requirements for your app:iOS 13.4 or later
