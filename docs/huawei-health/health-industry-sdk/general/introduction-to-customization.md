# Customization Description

Enterprises can call open enterprise customization capabilities through the government and enterprise customization service platforms of HEM and VMALL to meet device branding and marketing customization requirements. This enables enterprises to deploy customized content on Huawei industry wearables, including startup logos, startup animations, watch faces, HarmonyOS apps, and enterprise information.

**HEM Enterprise Customization**

Currently, the customization service platform of HEM supports the following industry wearables: B5 Series lite wearables (B5-536/B5-532/B5-736), HUAWEI WATCH H5 Series (H5546/H5556/H5756), H9 Series (H9D20), H3 Series (H3540), and B9 Series (B9-D10), B7 Series smart wearables (B7-536/B7-738), HUAWEI WATCH H7 Series (H7546), and smart bands (HA580/HA590/HA5A0). All these industry wearables support the startup logo, watch faces, and enterprise information, and the B7 and H7 Series smart watches also support pre-installed HarmonyOS apps, keepalive and automatic startup of pre-installed apps, startup animation, and more. The customization service platform of HEM features a streamlined interface, enabling enterprises to customize content more quickly and conveniently.

For details about the customization process and introduction, please refer to [HUAWEI Enterprise Manager](https://developer.huawei.com/business/en/doc/HEM/hem-introduction-0000001075229448).

**VMALL Enterprise Customization**

Currently, the customization service platform of VMALL supports the following industry wearables: B5 Series (B5-536/B5-532), HUAWEI WATCH H5 Series (H5546/H5556/H5756), H9 Series (H9D20), H3 Series (H3540), B9 Series (B9-D10), and bands (HA580/HA590/HA5A0). The platform features a standard customization process and feedback mechanism.
