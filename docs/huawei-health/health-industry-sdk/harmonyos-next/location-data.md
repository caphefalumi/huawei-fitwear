# Location Data

You can directly use the system APIs provided by HarmonyOS to implement related requirements. For details, please refer to the [overview of Location Kit](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/location-kit-intro).

> **Note:** People take their mobile devices wherever they go. Mobile devices have become a necessity in people's daily routines, whether it be for looking at the weather forecast, browsing news, hailing a taxi, navigating, or recording data from a workout. All these activities are so much associated with the location services on mobile devices. With the location awareness capability offered by HarmonyOS, mobile devices will be able to obtain real-time, accurate location data. Building location awareness into your app can also lead to a better contextual experience for app users. Your app can call location-specific APIs to obtain the location information of a mobile device for offering location-based services such as drive navigation and motion track recording. The location subsystem provides services using multiple positioning technologies, such as Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) positioning and network positioning (for example, cellular, WLAN, and Bluetooth positioning). Using these technologies, the device location can be accurately determined whether the user's device is indoors or outdoors. Coordinate A coordinate describes a location on the earth using the longitude and latitude in reference to the World Geodetic Coordinate System 1984. GNSS positioning GNSS positioning determines the location of a mobile device by using the positioning algorithms offered by the device chip to process location data from GNSS, for example, GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, and Galileo. The specific positioning system used during the process depends on the hardware capabilities of the device. Cellular positioning Cellular positioning estimates the current location of a mobile device based on the location of the resident cell tower in reference to the neighboring cell towers. This method offers relatively low accuracy and requires access to the cellular network. WLAN or Bluetooth positioning WLAN or Bluetooth positioning estimates the current location of a mobile device based on the locations of WLAN access points (APs) and Bluetooth devices that can be discovered by the device. The accuracy of this method depends on the density of fixed WLAN APs and Bluetooth devices around the device. A higher density can yield more accurate results than cellular positioning. This method also requires access to the network. In addition to basic location services, Location Kit also provides functionalities and APIs, such as geofencing and geocoding (both forward and reverse). A geofence is a virtual boundary around a geographical area. When a device enters or exits this area, automatic notifications and alarms will be received. Geocoding is the process of converting geographic entities (such as locations or elements) into codes. Forward geocoding: Obtains the longitude and latitude of a place based on its address. Reverse geocoding: Obtains the information of a place corresponding to given longitude and latitude.
