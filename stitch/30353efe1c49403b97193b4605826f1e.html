Core Kinetic Cue

Key Execution Steps

Lock shoulders down and set your lats tight like squeezing oranges.

check

Squeeze glutes at the top without hyper-extending the lower back.

Squatting the weight down instead of hip hinging. Keep shin angle locked to protect lumbar spine.
